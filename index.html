About

Who's in the file

I'm a BNSP-certified HR professional with a psychology degree and a habit of turning messy hiring processes into ones that hold up under an audit. Most of my work lives at the intersection of two things people rarely put together: full-cycle recruitment and the unglamorous back-office task of wiring company data into Indonesia's national regulatory platforms — SATU SEHAT, SISDMK, BPJS — so nothing falls through the cracks.

My track record sits mostly in healthcare and pharmacy, but the toolkit travels: structured psychological assessments that make hiring decisions less of a gut call, KPI frameworks that tie performance to something you can actually measure, and recruitment systems built to keep working after I've handed them off.

I'm currently looking for an HR Specialist, HR Generalist, or People Operations role — healthcare or otherwise — where data-driven HR practices and compliance know-how can genuinely move the business forward.
